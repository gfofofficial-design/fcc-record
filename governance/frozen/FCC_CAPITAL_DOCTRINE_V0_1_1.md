# FCC CAPITAL DOCTRINE v0.1.1 — FROZEN
The rules FCC must obey, fixed before any results exist · spec only · no implementation · 2026-08-17
Status: **FROZEN 2026-08-17** upon owner + ChatGPT final adversarial review — governing Stage-0 FCC document, subordinate to FCC Product Blueprint v1.1.1 (FROZEN). Ratifications R-1…R-10 and correction S-2's doctrine ripples are final as applied. Amendable only via §P (prospective, checkpoint-frozen); §B2's permanent exclusion remains outside §P entirely.
Drafting assumption, per the gate instruction: future FCC may be motivated to protect its record. Every rule below is written against that FCC.

## §A · SCOPE AND SUPREMACY
A1. This Doctrine governs every FCC Capital Instrument and every FCC institutional action of the classes named in §F. Where Doctrine is silent, the Blueprint's guardrails control; where both are silent, the action requires an owner decision logged as a process instrument.
A2. Doctrine binds FCC before it binds anyone else. FCC's own compliance is scored (PROCESS dimension, §M) on every instrument.

## §B · INSTRUMENT ELIGIBILITY AND EXCLUSIONS
B1. Eligible instrument types at Stage 0: **qualification-stance instruments** (a falsifiable would/would-not-qualify judgment about a protocol, asset, venue, or structure under a cited Doctrine section, resolved by a pre-registered observable) and **process instruments** (FCC institutional decisions: Doctrine adoption/amendment, gate definitions, exclusion rulings, mandate events). Allocation-class instruments (sized positions) are ineligible until the Stage 0.5 gate (§S) opens.
B2. **Permanent exclusions.** FCC never files an instrument whose subject is $GFOF or any Federation-issued asset — "Federation-issued" means any token, NFT, or on-chain instrument created or controlled by GFOF, Dossier, FCC, or any Federation entity, including the Founding Member NFT. This exclusion is permanent and not amendable by §P procedure; removing it requires a new blueprint cycle with owner + ChatGPT + counsel.
B3. Prohibited subjects regardless of type: assets or structures whose evaluation would require non-public information; any subject where a §E conflict cannot be cured by disclosure; any instrument whose resolution FCC or a disclosed insider can unilaterally influence (self-fulfilling instruments) — such influence risk must be assessed in the AI counter-thesis and is challengeable as "manipulable resolution criterion."
B4. **Triviality bar.** An instrument qualifies only if material uncertainty exists: the AI counter-thesis (§R) must articulate at least one plausible failure path. An instrument for which the standing adversary cannot construct one is flagged TRIVIAL, may still be filed for completeness, but is excluded from headline OUTCOME hit-rate and counted in a separate published TRIVIAL tally. (RATIFIED R-10. Closes easy-instrument cherry-picking in part; residual topic-selection discretion is addressed in §F3, ratified for Stage 0 under R-9.)

## §C · EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS
C1. Every evidence item cited in a locked body must be published and publicly accessible at LOCK. Dossier evidence: **published Dossier outputs only** (§Q firewall).
C2. **Content-addressing.** Static documents are cited with a SHA-256 of the retrieved content at LOCK (evidence substitution is thereby detectable). Live data sources are cited as source + exact query + snapshot value + retrieval timestamp.
C3. Post-lock evidence changes occur only through an **evidence-defect disclosure annex** plus a simultaneous FCC corrections entry; the original citation stays in the locked body forever.
C4. Minimum evidence floor: an instrument must cite at least the evidence the thesis logically depends on; the AI lint (§R2) checks dependency coverage and the "missing evidence" challenge category polices it after lock.

## §D · RESOLUTION SOURCES
D1. Doctrine maintains a public, versioned **Source Registry** of acceptable resolution-source classes, in priority order: (1) direct on-chain data; (2) official first-party APIs/documents; (3) named reputable aggregators. Registry additions are Doctrine amendments (§P) and apply prospectively only. (Closes favorable-source selection: sources are chosen from a pre-committed registry, named at LOCK, and policed by the "bad benchmark"/"manipulable resolution criterion" categories.)
D2. Each criterion names its primary source and, where one exists in the Registry, a fallback source, both at LOCK.
D3. Criteria must be stated so that a third party can execute the evaluation mechanically: named source, exact query or measurement, exact comparator, exact horizon timestamp handling. The AI lint must emit a deterministic evaluation-procedure statement; an instrument failing that lint cannot lock. (Closes vague criteria at the gate rather than in argument.)

## §E · CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST RULES
E1. Every instrument carries a mandatory conflicts/disclosures field: any position, relationship, or interest of FCC, its operator(s), or the Federation treasury in the instrument's subject, existing at LOCK, must be disclosed there. An empty field is an affirmative statement of no conflicts.
E2. **No undisclosed positioning during OPEN.** FCC operators may not open, close, or materially change positions in an instrument's subject between LOCK and TERMINAL without a dated disclosure annex filed before or with the action. (Exploit found in drafting: an operator could trade against or ahead of FCC's own public record; closed here.)
E3. A conflict discovered post-lock that existed at LOCK and was undisclosed is a filing-time defect (§N2 → INVALIDATED-BY-DOCTRINE). A conflict arising after LOCK is disclosed by annex and does not invalidate.
E4. $GFOF/Federation-asset conflicts cannot be cured by disclosure; they are excluded subjects (§B2).

## §F · DECISION PROCEDURE, QUALIFICATION, AND THE COMPLETENESS RULE
F1. Stage-0 filing authority: the FCC operator (owner). Every DRAFT passes AI Doctrine-lint (§R2) before LOCK; lint output is preserved and published with the instrument.
F2. **Completeness rule (closes selective filing).** If FCC publishes a Capital Assessment or any public capital opinion, it MUST carry a qualification-stance instrument — no unscored opinions. Every institutional decision in these classes MUST be filed as a process instrument: Doctrine adoption and amendment; gate openings/closings; mandate events; suspension/halt/kill events; challenge-protocol changes; source-registry changes.
F3. **Intent registration (closes silent abandonment).** Opening work on an assessment is itself registered in a public Filing Log at the time work begins (intent → timestamp). Assessments later abandoned are closed in the log with a reason; the abandonment count is a published statistic. Residual discretion — *which topics FCC chooses to assess at all* — cannot be fully closed by rule; mitigations: published topic-selection rationale per assessment and a public proposal queue with published triage. RATIFIED FOR STAGE 0 (R-9): the residual discretion is acceptable with the Filing Log, per-assessment rationale, proposal queue, and published triage; FCC is not required to assess every well-formed community proposal. This ratification is revisited before Stage 1 (§T1).
F4. Qualification standards for locking: lint pass (deterministic criteria, dependency coverage, benchmark-menu compliance, conflicts field present, ordering rule satisfiable) + triviality bar (§B4) + eligibility (§B).

## §G · BENCHMARK MENU AND SELECTION
G1. The menu exists before any instrument and is amendable only prospectively (§P). Menu classes: **Null baseline** — hold the instrument's denomination asset (SOL-denominated → hold SOL; stable-denominated → hold that stable). **Passive comparator** — a mechanical, named-source, market-cap-weighted basket of the instrument's declared sector, composition snapshotted at LOCK.
G2. Selection is mechanical by instrument type: allocation-class instruments (Stage 0.5+) require both menu classes. Qualification-stance instruments require benchmarks only where the resolution criterion is comparative; otherwise the benchmark fields are N/A by rule, not by choice.
G3. Off-menu benchmarks are prohibited; using one is a filing-time defect. Menu misapplication is challengeable ("bad benchmark"). (Closes benchmark shopping.)

## §H · SIZING AND CONCENTRATION FRAMEWORK
H1. Stage 0 instruments carry no sizing; fields are N/A.
H2. The framework that will bind Stage 0.5+ is structural now, numerical later: per-position cap as a percentage of book; per-sector concentration cap; a drawdown-suspension trigger measured from book high-water mark that automatically suspends new instruments pending review. The numbers are set at the Stage 0.5 gate by owner ratification with published rationale — fixing them now would be false precision with no book in existence. Deferral RATIFIED (R-6).

## §I · CHALLENGE WINDOWS AND RESPONSE WINDOWS
I1. **Challenge window = the entire OPEN state.** Challenges may be filed from LOCK until resolution evaluation begins. No separate window number is needed — this is derived, not chosen.
I2. **Minimum OPEN period.** No instrument may begin resolution earlier than **72 hours after LOCK**, so a challenge opportunity always exists even for short-horizon criteria. Derivation: 72 hours is the Federation's established public-consideration convention (Realms governance proposals run 72 hours). RATIFIED (R-1).
I3. **FCC response window — RATIFIED (R-2): 7 calendar days from receipt of a well-formed challenge.** Earlier response is permitted. Breach is an automatic PROCESS violation on the instrument (§M) — challenge suppression is self-harming by construction.

## §J · CHALLENGE-RESOLUTION LOGIC — ALL EIGHT CATEGORIES
Each resolves UPHELD / PARTIALLY UPHELD / NOT UPHELD against the stated test; "partially" means the defect is real but not dispositive for the instrument's qualification or resolution.
J1. **Flawed premise** — upheld if a factual premise the thesis logically depends on is shown false as of LOCK.
J2. **Missing evidence** — upheld if an evidence item the thesis logically depends on was absent and, once obtained, changes the instrument's §F4 qualification or its stated risk materially.
J3. **Ignored risk** — upheld if a risk of a class the risk statement was required to address (per lint dependency coverage) was omitted and that class of risk is material to the thesis.
J4. **Inappropriate sizing** — (Stage 0.5+) upheld if sizing breaches §H or is indefensible under the instrument's own stated risk.
J5. **Bad benchmark** — upheld if the benchmark selection violates §G mechanics or the comparator is not the menu-mandated one.
J6. **Conflict of interest** — upheld if a §E1-disclosable interest existed at LOCK and was not disclosed (also triggers §N2), or an OPEN-period action breached §E2.
J7. **Manipulable resolution criterion** — upheld if any party FCC controls or a disclosed insider can materially influence the resolution measurement.
J8. **Invalid assumption** — upheld if a stated assumption is shown internally inconsistent or contrary to evidence available at LOCK.
J9. Procedure: one challenge per category per identity per instrument; duplicates consolidate with first-filer attribution by receipt timestamp; malformed filings are returned unscored under published triage rules with a public receipt (nothing is silently dropped — closes challenge suppression); FCC resolves against this section's tests; one meta-challenge round per resolution; an unresolved dispute sets the DISPUTED flag (§K). A dismissed challenge later vindicated by the outcome is logged as an FCC process miss on FCC's corrections log.
J10. FCC may file a **self-noted defect** (same taxonomy, same public record) against its own instrument; concession (§N4) requires an upheld challenge — self-noted or external — as its basis. (Closes silent quiet-fix and bounds strategic concession.)

## §K · DISPUTED-FLAG RULES
K1. The flag is set when a meta-challenge round ends without agreement. It never alters the terminal disposition, which evaluates strictly against locked criteria.
K2. The dissent annex is permanent; the count of DISPUTED-flag instruments is a first-class published statistic displayed with all aggregates.

## §L · OUTCOME SCORING
L1. OUTCOME ∈ {CORRECT, INCORRECT, UNSCORED}, evaluated mechanically against the locked criteria via the locked evaluation procedure and named sources.
L2. Disposition mapping (frozen v1.1.1, restated as rule): RESOLVED-CORRECT → CORRECT · RESOLVED-INCORRECT → INCORRECT · CONCEDED → INCORRECT · VOID → UNSCORED · INVALIDATED-BY-DOCTRINE → UNSCORED (with §N3 informational outcome).
L3. Headline hit-rate = CORRECT / (CORRECT + INCORRECT), TRIVIAL-flagged instruments excluded from both terms and tallied separately.

## §M · PROCESS SCORING
M1. PROCESS ∈ {COMPLIANT, VIOLATED}, assessed per instrument against: disclosure (§E), ordering (Spec lock semantics), evidence (§C), conflict conduct (§E2), benchmark mechanics (§G), response windows (§I3), annex integrity (Spec), completeness obligations (§F2) attributable to that instrument.
M2. Violations divide into **filing-time defects** (corrupt the pre-registration itself: ordering breach, off-menu benchmark, §E3 undisclosed lock-time conflict, sizing breach at filing, criteria that were never §D3-evaluable) → the instrument terminates INVALIDATED-BY-DOCTRINE; and **conduct violations** (late response, §E2 breach cured by disclosure, annex-procedure faults) → the instrument remains valid, PROCESS = VIOLATED, disposition unaffected. A correct outcome never erases either kind.
M3. Process-violation rate is computed over ALL instruments and published with every aggregate.

## §N · TERMINAL DISPOSITION TREATMENTS
N1. **VOID** — declarable only when the primary AND any named fallback source have genuinely ceased to be queryable for the criterion; requires a public explanation annex; prohibited while any named source remains queryable; where partial data survives, the computable informational outcome is published. VOID figures are displayed adjacent to every hit-rate as numerator, denominator, and absolute count beside the percentage — never the percentage alone. **RATIFIED (R-7): a VOID rate above 10% of terminal instruments triggers a mandatory public Doctrine review** — a review obligation, not an automatic kill. (Closes convenient VOID.)
N2. **INVALIDATED-BY-DOCTRINE** — filing-time defects only (§M2); requires a corrections entry naming the defect; PROCESS = VIOLATED by definition.
N3. **Anti-erasure rule.** Where an INVALIDATED instrument's locked criteria remain measurable at horizon, the informational outcome is computed and published on the instrument and in a separate published count. Invalidation can never make a looming miss disappear.
N4. **CONCEDED** — requires an upheld challenge (§J10) identifying a dispositive defect; terminates early against FCC with challenger credit; OUTCOME = INCORRECT in every statistic. Structural note: because concession and RESOLVED-INCORRECT score identically on OUTCOME, strategic concession buys nothing in the numbers; the only path it could launder is reputational, and requiring an upheld challenge as basis closes self-serving concessions without a real defect.

## §O · AGGREGATE REPORTING RULES
O1. Fixed before results (this section is scoring-relevant and freezes between checkpoints per §P3): every instrument appears in exactly one terminal-disposition count; hit-rate per §L3; concessions counted as misses; process-violation rate over all instruments; VOID (as numerator/denominator/absolute count per §N1), INVALIDATED, TRIVIAL, and DISPUTED counts always displayed alongside hit-rates, never footnoted; the **anchored-precedence share** (Spec §3: fraction of terminal instruments whose ordering is independently proven at the ANCHORED layer) published with every aggregate, so any pattern of independently-unproven ordering is visible; abandonment count from the Filing Log; challenge statistics (filed, upheld, partial, not-upheld, FCC-process-miss count from J9); no annualization, no window selection, no survivor edits; the full instrument list is always one click from any aggregate figure.

## §P · DOCTRINE AMENDMENT PROCEDURE
P1. Amendments are proposed publicly, Council-visible (advisory now, binding per the governance spec's own migration), owner-ratified, and filed as process instruments.
P2. **Prospective only.** No amendment applies to any instrument locked before the amendment's effective time. (Closes post-hoc rule-fitting.)
P3. **Checkpoint freeze.** Sections governing scoring, benchmarks, aggregation, gates, and kill criteria (§G, §L, §M, §N, §O, §S, §T, §U, §V) may not be amended between the opening of an evaluation window and the publication of its results. (Closes amendment-after-seeing-results.)
P4. §B2's permanent exclusion is outside this procedure entirely.

## §Q · DOSSIER / FCC FIREWALL
Q1. FCC cites only published Dossier outputs; no early access, no lookahead. Q2. Dossier's measurement agenda is set independently; scoping a measurement to fit a pending instrument is a firewall violation. Q3. A formal FCC measurement request is published before results exist. Q4. New Dossier evidence that invalidates an open instrument resolves against FCC under the ordinary rules — it is never contested by pressuring the evidence function. Q5. Any firewall violation is corrections-logged; an uncorrectable structural violation triggers the capture kill criterion (§V).

## §R · AI AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES
R1. AI is the standing adversary: a counter-thesis annex is MANDATORY on every instrument, generated under a published, versioned Standing Adversary Procedure; changing that procedure is a §P amendment (prospective). The full output is published unedited and labeled ADVERSARIAL REVIEW // AI; humans may add adversarial notes, never remove or soften AI output; an instrument is ineligible for resolution until its counter-thesis annex exists, and its absence at resolution is a PROCESS violation. (Closes counter-thesis weakening/selective presentation.)
R2. AI Doctrine-lint runs pre-lock (criteria determinism, dependency coverage, menu compliance, conflicts field, ordering satisfiability); lint output is published with the instrument.
R3. AI is prohibited from: filing, locking, or resolving instruments; resolving challenges; holding or authorizing capital in any amount at any stage; generating evidence; producing unlabeled content; auto-executing any action at Stage 1+.

## §S · STAGE 0.5 GATE (SIMULATION)
S1. Opens only when all hold: (a) **format stability by coverage** — at least one instrument has reached each of RESOLVED-CORRECT, RESOLVED-INCORRECT, and one of {CONCEDED, VOID, INVALIDATED-BY-DOCTRINE}, and at least one challenge has fully resolved, with zero breaking schema amendments during that span (derived: the format is proven when each state-machine path has executed; no raw count is invented — coverage basis RATIFIED, R-4); (b) Doctrine v0.1 published with comment period closed; (c) simulation reporting rules fixed before any results; (d) counsel review of hypothetical/simulated-performance presentation completed. All v1.1.1 simulation honesty constraints apply thereafter.

## §T · STAGE 1 GATE (TREASURY SELF-MANDATE)
T1. In order: Doctrine ratified → §S coverage met → **FCC EVALUATION METHODOLOGY v0.1 published and frozen (§T2)** → the methodology's minimum evidence requirement satisfied AND the OUTCOME record ≥ its null baselines under that methodology at a quarterly checkpoint (§V1) → adversarial review demonstrably operating (resolved challenges exist) → R-9 topic-discretion ratification revisited → Council/owner ratification of the specific mandate: scope, caps, automatic drawdown-suspension triggers, emergency-halt procedure → legal review of mandate structure and wrapper → **Stage-1 precedence attestation mechanism specified and ratified (Spec §3.8 — currently GATED)** → grant recorded as a corrections entry and a process instrument. **Until the Evaluation Methodology exists, Stage 1 is closed; no owner-selected instrument count substitutes for it (R-5 reframe).**
T2. **FCC EVALUATION METHODOLOGY v0.1 — required future artifact (gate established here; not written in this pass).** Before Stage-1 eligibility can be evaluated, FCC must publish and freeze a pre-registered statistical/evaluation methodology that derives and justifies its own minimum evidence requirement. It must address at minimum: sample size; instrument independence and correlation; base rates; benchmark comparison; uncertainty and confidence; treatment of concessions; TRIVIAL exclusions; VOID and INVALIDATED treatment; and the conditions under which the only honest conclusion is INSUFFICIENT HISTORY. The methodology freezes before it is applied and is amendable only under §P (prospective, checkpoint-frozen).

## §U · SUSPENSION AND HALT PROCEDURE
U1. Automatic suspension of new instruments on: any kill-criterion trigger (§V); the §H drawdown trigger (Stage 0.5+); discovery of an integrity-class defect pending investigation. U2. Suspension and lift are process instruments with public reasons. U3. Open instruments continue to resolution during suspension — suspension stops new filings, never the scoring of existing ones (a halt can never shield open instruments from their outcomes).

## §V · KILL CRITERIA (halt-and-record; thresholds fixed here or ratified before results)
V1. **Judgment failure** — evaluation checkpoints are **quarterly (RATIFIED R-3)**, aligned with the Federation's existing #006 quarterly convention. **Condition (R-3):** judgment-failure scoring does not activate until the §T2 Evaluation Methodology's minimum evidence standard is satisfied; every checkpoint before that publishes **INSUFFICIENT HISTORY — never PASS**. Once active: the OUTCOME record (concessions as misses) failing both null baselines under the frozen methodology → halt + public measurement review; a clean PROCESS record does not excuse it.
V2. **Arena death** — validation window **RATIFIED (R-8): 180 days.** **Minimum outreach obligations, which must be performed and logged as a process instrument before the clock begins:** the Challenge Protocol and a public how-to-challenge document published on both Federation properties; announcement on the Federation's established public channels (@GFOF_Offcial, @Dossier_HQ, t.me/GFOF_SOL); every instrument filing announced on at least one of those channels; the proposal queue visibly open with published triage. If those obligations were not performed, FCC may not claim the arena test was valid — the window has not run. No genuine external challenge participation within a validly-run 180-day window → no advancement to §S/§T; one public retooling permitted, else halt.
V3. **Integrity breach** — back-dating, hash manipulation, or evidence fabrication → immediate halt and full disclosure; no cure inside FCC v1.
V4. **Regulatory block** — counsel determines a core element cannot proceed → the affected stage is cancelled; if Stage 0 itself is blocked, FCC halts.
V5. **Capture** — §Q violation not structurally correctable → halt.
V6. Every kill event is a corrections entry and a process instrument; "kill" means halt-and-record, never deletion.

## §W · CORRECTIONS PROCEDURE
W1. FCC maintains its own append-only corrections log under the Federation's established discipline (append-only, status-labeled, periodically snapshot-anchored). W2. Mandatory entries: evidence-defect disclosures; invalidations; process violations; challenge-resolution misses (J9); firewall violations; suspensions/halts/kills; gate transitions; amendment adoptions. W3. First entry at launch: the publish-either-way pre-commitment.

## §X · PUBLIC REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
X1. Always public: the Doctrine (versioned), the Source Registry, the benchmark menu, the Filing Log, every instrument with all annexes, all aggregates per §O, the corrections log, the Standing Adversary Procedure version in force. X2. Honest null states everywhere ("—", "insufficient history"); no figure is displayed that cannot be recomputed from the public record.
