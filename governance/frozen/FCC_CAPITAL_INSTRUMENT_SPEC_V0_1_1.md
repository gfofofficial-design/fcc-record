# FCC CAPITAL INSTRUMENT SPECIFICATION v0.1.1 — FROZEN
Technology-neutral, implementable specification of the frozen Capital Instrument · spec only · no implementation · 2026-08-17
Status: **FROZEN 2026-08-17** upon owner + ChatGPT final adversarial review — governing Stage-0 FCC document, subordinate to FCC Product Blueprint v1.1.1 (FROZEN), companion to Capital Doctrine v0.1.1 (FROZEN). S-1/S-3/S-4 ratifications and the S-2 precedence-evidence correction are final as applied. §3.8's Stage-1 attestation gate and Doctrine §T2's Evaluation Methodology gate remain closed. Design priority, per the gate: independent third-party verifiability over interface convenience.

## 1 · CANONICAL SCHEMA
An instrument is a structured record with two byte-distinct regions: the **LOCKED BODY** (immutable after FILED/LOCKED) and the **ANNEX LOG** (append-only). Field table — R = required, O = optional, C = conditional:

**Locked body**
`instrument_id` (string, R) — permanent identifier, assigned at DRAFT, never reused.
`instrument_type` (enum, R) — `qualification-stance` | `process` | `allocation` (allocation ineligible until Doctrine §S opens).
`decision` (string, R) — the decision in one falsifiable sentence.
`thesis` (string, R) — the reasoning the decision rests on.
`evidence_chain` (array, R) — items of `{source_url, retrieved_at, content_sha256 (C: static documents), query (C: live sources), snapshot_value (C: live sources), note}` per Doctrine §C.
`risk_statement` (string, R) — how this fails and what is lost.
`sizing` (object, C) — N/A for Stage-0 types; for `allocation`: `{book_pct, sector, denomination}` under Doctrine §H.
`horizon` (timestamp, R) — the resolution evaluation time (UTC).
`resolution_criteria` (array, R) — items of `{criterion_id, statement, comparator, threshold, evaluation_procedure}` — each machine-evaluable per Doctrine §D3.
`resolution_sources` (array, R) — items of `{criterion_id, primary_source, fallback_source (O), registry_class}` from the Doctrine §D Source Registry.
`benchmarks` (object, C) — `{null_baseline, passive_comparator, composition_snapshot}` per Doctrine §G; N/A by rule for non-comparative stance instruments.
`conflicts_disclosures` (array, R) — may be empty; an empty array is an affirmative no-conflict statement per Doctrine §E1.
`base_rate_note` (string, O) — naive base-rate estimate where estimable (Doctrine §B4 context).
`doctrine_version` (string, R) — the Doctrine version in force at LOCK.
`filed_at` (timestamp, R) — set at the FILED/LOCKED event.
`schema_version` (string, R).

**Envelope (outside the locked bytes, verifiable against them)**
`lock_sha256` (string, R) — SHA-256 of the canonical serialization of the locked body.
`ots_anchor` (object, R) — `{status: requested|confirmed|failed, requested_at, confirmed_at (C), proof_ref (C)}`.
`state` (enum, R) — `DRAFT | FILED_LOCKED | OPEN | TERMINAL`.
`terminal_disposition` (enum, C) — `RESOLVED-CORRECT | RESOLVED-INCORRECT | CONCEDED | VOID | INVALIDATED-BY-DOCTRINE`. **No additional disposition may be introduced without returning to owner + ChatGPT.**
`outcome` (enum, C) — `CORRECT | INCORRECT | UNSCORED`.
`process` (enum, C) — `COMPLIANT | VIOLATED`.
`informational_outcome` (enum, C) — published where Doctrine §N3 (INVALIDATED, measurable) or §N1 (VOID, partial data) applies.
`ordering_evidence` (object, R) — `{level: SELF-REPORTED | PUBLISHED | ANCHORED, publication_ref (C), anchor_blocktime (C), proven_before (C: the earliest independently established time by which the lock demonstrably existed)}` — populated per §3.4–3.8; the strongest independently established layer only, never an aspiration.
`disputed` (bool, R, default false).
`trivial` (bool, R, default false) — per Doctrine §B4.
`annexes` (array, R) — the annex log (§4).
`corrections_refs` (array, R) — FCC corrections-log entries touching this instrument.

Semantics rule: nothing outside the locked body may contradict it; envelope fields are derived by rule (state machine, Doctrine mappings) and every derivation is recomputable by a third party from the public record.

## 2 · STATE MACHINE
`DRAFT → FILED/LOCKED → OPEN → TERMINAL` — exactly as frozen; no other states, no skipping, no reversal.
DRAFT: internal, no standing, may be edited or discarded freely; discarded drafts of registered assessments close their Filing Log entries with a reason (Doctrine §F3).
FILED/LOCKED: single event — canonicalization, hash, timestamp, anchor request (§3). Entry condition: Doctrine §F4 qualification (lint pass + eligibility + triviality assessment recorded).
OPEN: challenge window (the whole state, Doctrine §I1); annexes accrue; minimum duration 72h before resolution may begin (Doctrine §I2).
TERMINAL: exactly one disposition from the frozen five; requires the AI counter-thesis annex to exist (Doctrine §R1), all well-formed challenges resolved or their windows closed, resolution + benchmark annexes recorded, OUTCOME/PROCESS populated per the frozen mapping.

## 3 · LOCK SEMANTICS
3.1 **Locked bytes.** The locked body is the exhaustive field set in §1 "Locked body," serialized canonically. Everything else is envelope or annex and is never covered by `lock_sha256`.
3.2 **Canonical serialization.** UTF-8 JSON; object keys sorted lexicographically at every depth; no insignificant whitespace; numbers in shortest round-trip form; timestamps as RFC 3339 UTC (`Z`). **RATIFIED (S-1): RFC 8785 (JSON Canonicalization Scheme) is the normative canonical serialization standard.** The requirement stands that two independent implementations produce identical bytes; RFC 8785 conformance is the test.
3.3 **Hash + timestamp.** `lock_sha256` = SHA-256 over those bytes, computed at the FILED/LOCKED event; `filed_at` is inside the locked body and must equal the event time to tolerance stated in the published procedure.
3.4 **Precedence-evidence layers (S-2 correction).** FCC's own clocks prove nothing to anyone else. The specification distinguishes three layers and never conflates them:
— **Layer 0 · SELF-REPORTED:** `filed_at` and `requested_at` are FCC event timestamps. They are claims. **`requested_at` is not, by itself, treated as proof of anything** — not of lock time, not of ordering.
— **Layer 1 · PUBLISHED:** immediately at lock, `lock_sha256` is published to an independently observable public channel (its reference recorded in `ordering_evidence.publication_ref`). This establishes precedence only as strongly as that channel's own timestamping is trusted by a verifier — e.g., a hosting provider's commit timestamp is the provider's attestation, observable but not cryptographic.
— **Layer 2 · ANCHORED:** OTS/Bitcoin confirmation proves cryptographically that `lock_sha256` existed **no later than** the anchoring block's time (`anchor_blocktime`). It proves an upper bound on creation time. It does not prove the lock occurred at `requested_at`, and it proves nothing about the interval between request and confirmation.
**What each layer can establish about ordering:** to demonstrate the lock preceded an event at time T — SELF-REPORTED: nothing, to a third party; PUBLISHED: precedence if `publication time ≤ T` and the verifier accepts the channel's attestation; ANCHORED: precedence, cryptographically, iff `anchor_blocktime ≤ T`. `ordering_evidence.level` records the strongest layer actually established, with `proven_before` set to the corresponding independently verifiable time. **An ordering that is not independently established is represented as not independently established — on the instrument, in aggregates (the anchored-precedence share, Doctrine §O), and in every rendering. The system never displays unproven ordering as proven.**
3.5 **Anchoring procedure and failure.** The anchor request is submitted immediately at lock and Layer-1 publication occurs in the same operation; `requested_at`, `publication_ref` recorded; `confirmed_at`/`proof_ref`/`anchor_blocktime` recorded when Bitcoin confirms. While unconfirmed, every rendering shows `anchor: pending` and `ordering_evidence.level` remains at PUBLISHED. The instrument may progress through OPEN but may not reach TERMINAL until the anchor is confirmed. On permanent anchoring failure: evidence-defect-class disclosure annex + corrections entry + fresh re-anchor of the same `lock_sha256`; the instrument's precedence remains Layer-1 for the affected interval and is labeled as such permanently.
3.6 **Ordering rules by instrument class (Stage 0).** Stance instruments: evidence snapshot times ≤ `filed_at` (a self-consistency requirement inside the claim), and the resolution measurement occurs at/after `horizon`. The ordering that matters — *the claim existed before the outcome was knowable* — is **independently proven iff `anchor_blocktime` precedes the resolution measurement time**; the 72-hour minimum OPEN period (Doctrine §I2) ordinarily gives anchoring ample time to confirm first. Where it has not (network conditions), the instrument still terminates on its objective criteria, but `ordering_evidence.level = PUBLISHED` and the instrument is permanently rendered with **PRECEDENCE: NOT INDEPENDENTLY PROVEN**; a timely anchor request keeps PROCESS compliant, an untimely one is a conduct violation (Doctrine §M2). A true ordering breach — a dependent element predating the lock — remains a filing-time defect.
3.7 **Third-party verification procedure.** (1) Fetch the instrument JSON; (2) extract the locked body; (3) canonicalize per RFC 8785 and compute SHA-256; (4) compare to `lock_sha256`; (5) verify the OTS proof against Bitcoin, establishing `anchor_blocktime` — the latest time by which the lock existed (an upper bound, nothing more); (6) check `ordering_evidence`: confirm the recorded level is justified by the evidence (anchor vs publication vs neither) and that `proven_before` precedes the resolution measurement where ANCHORED is claimed; (7) re-run each `evaluation_procedure` against the named sources and compare to the resolution annex; (8) recompute OUTCOME/PROCESS from the frozen mappings and the annex log; (9) confirm every annex hash and timestamp and that no annex claims to modify locked text. A discrepancy at any step is publicly demonstrable without trusting FCC.
3.8 **Stage-1 precedence attestation — GATED.** For real-capital instruments, the dependent capital action may follow the lock by minutes; Layer-2 confirmation takes hours, and Layer-1 is attestation-grade, not cryptographic. **The present mechanism therefore cannot prove lock-before-action for temporally tight sequences, and this specification does not pretend it can.** Stage-1 instruments require an attestation mechanism that independently demonstrates the locked commitment existed before the dependent capital action (candidate direction, not selected here: committing `lock_sha256` on the same chain as the action, before it, using that chain's own ordering). That mechanism must be specified and ratified before Stage 1 opens (Doctrine §T1). Until then, Stage-1 attestation is explicitly GATED.

## 4 · ANNEX PROTOCOL
Annexes are immutable once appended; each carries `{annex_id, annex_type, author_class (FCC|AI|external identity), body, appended_at, annex_sha256}`. Allowed types and timing:
`ai-counter-thesis` — OPEN only; mandatory exactly one per instrument (supplements allowed as `ai-counter-thesis-supplement`); published unedited (Doctrine §R1).
`challenge` — OPEN only, before resolution evaluation begins (§5).
`fcc-response` — OPEN, within the ratified response window of the challenge it answers.
`evidence-defect-disclosure` — OPEN or TERMINAL; requires a simultaneous corrections entry; never edits the locked citation.
`challenge-resolution` — at TERMINAL evaluation (or earlier where dispositive per Doctrine §J); one meta-challenge round may follow; then final.
`terminal-resolution` — the mechanical evaluation record: per-criterion source readings, comparator results, disposition derivation.
`benchmark-result` — with terminal-resolution where benchmarks apply.
`correction-reference` — any state; links an FCC corrections entry.
`post-terminal-annotation` — TERMINAL only; commentary that changes nothing; must reference a corrections entry if it asserts any defect.
Integrity rule: no annex of any type may restate locked text with alterations; an annex quoting the locked body must quote it byte-exactly or link it. Annex ordering is by `appended_at`; the annex log itself is append-only and covered by the record's periodic snapshot anchoring.

## 5 · CHALLENGE SCHEMA
`{challenge_id, instrument_id, identity {handle, pubkey_or_account, registered_at}, category (enum of the frozen eight: flawed-premise | missing-evidence | ignored-risk | inappropriate-sizing | bad-benchmark | conflict-of-interest | manipulable-resolution-criterion | invalid-assumption), claim, supporting_evidence (same item shape as §1 evidence), filed_at, receipt_id, status (FILED | CONSOLIDATED | RETURNED-MALFORMED | PENDING | UPHELD | PARTIALLY-UPHELD | NOT-UPHELD), consolidated_into (C), first_filer (bool, by receipt timestamp), resolution {resolved_at, rationale, doctrine_test_ref}, meta_challenge_ref (C, max one round), reputation_delta_ref (C)}`.
Identity — **RATIFIED (S-3): both wallet-signed identities and registered-account identities are supported. They are separate reputation identities unless an explicit, verifiable linking procedure binds them (a signed statement from the wallet naming the account, confirmed from the account); the system never infers that two identities belong to the same person.** One challenge per category per identity per instrument; duplicates consolidate with first-filer attribution; every filing, including malformed, receives a public timestamped receipt; reputation effects per Doctrine §J and the frozen non-financial rules.

## 6 · DUAL SCORING ENCODING
`outcome` and `process` are independent envelope fields, populated only at TERMINAL, per the frozen mapping (Doctrine §L2/§M): RESOLVED-CORRECT → CORRECT/independent · RESOLVED-INCORRECT → INCORRECT/independent · CONCEDED → INCORRECT/independent · VOID → UNSCORED/independent · INVALIDATED-BY-DOCTRINE → UNSCORED/VIOLATED. `informational_outcome` is REQUIRED on an INVALIDATED instrument whose locked criteria remain measurable (Doctrine §N3) and on a VOID instrument with computable partial data (§N1) — published on the instrument and in the separate aggregate count, excluded from official hit-rate. Aggregates are recomputable from the instrument set alone; no aggregate may exist that cannot be so recomputed (Doctrine §X2).

## 7 · VERIFICATION SUMMARY (the property that matters)
From the public record alone, an independent party can establish: what FCC claimed (locked body); when it locked it (OTS against Bitcoin); that it was never rewritten (hash + canonicalization); what evidence existed (content-addressed citations); what challenges arrived and when (receipts + annex log); what happened (terminal-resolution readings from named sources); whether the disposition follows the locked criteria (recompute §3.7 step 6); and whether FCC followed Doctrine (PROCESS recomputation from the annex log + Doctrine tests). Every dispute this system can host reduces to bytes, hashes, timestamps, and named-source readings. Publication surface (hosting architecture) is DEFERRED per S-4: this specification requires public availability and independent verifiability of every artifact above and deliberately does not choose where they are hosted.

## TEST FIXTURES — schema/protocol walkthroughs on paper only
These are protocol test fixtures, not public FCC instruments, not investment recommendations, not simulated performance, and not additions to any record. Subjects are hypothetical.

**FIXTURE A — clean correct.** Type `qualification-stance`; decision: "Hypothetical protocol ALPHA-TEST would not qualify under Doctrine §B/§E-class liquidity standards; predicted observable: its primary pool depth per the named registry source remains below the cited floor at horizon H." DRAFT → lint pass (criteria deterministic, dependency coverage met, conflicts array empty-affirmative, benchmarks N/A non-comparative) → FILED/LOCKED (canonical bytes hashed; anchor requested; `filed_at` set) → OPEN ≥72h; `ai-counter-thesis` annex filed (plausible failure path articulated → not TRIVIAL); zero challenges → horizon; anchor confirmed; terminal-resolution annex records the named-source reading below the floor → **TERMINAL: RESOLVED-CORRECT · OUTCOME=CORRECT · PROCESS=COMPLIANT · disputed=false**. Deterministic ✓.

**FIXTURE B — clean incorrect with an upheld challenge.** Same type; decision predicts observable X at H. During OPEN, external identity files `challenge` category `ignored-risk` with a receipt; FCC files `fcc-response` inside the ratified window, contesting; challenge status PENDING. At horizon the criterion fails — the risk the challenger named materialized. Terminal-resolution annex records the readings; `challenge-resolution` annex applies Doctrine §J3's test → **UPHELD**, first-filer reputation credit; no meta-challenge → **TERMINAL: RESOLVED-INCORRECT · OUTCOME=INCORRECT · PROCESS=COMPLIANT** (response window met; the omitted risk was outside lint's required dependency classes, so no §M violation — the miss is a judgment failure, not a process failure) · challenger vindicated on the permanent record; FCC's contest was wrong on substance, which the record shows without a process penalty. Deterministic ✓. (Had FCC instead conceded on the challenge pre-horizon after upholding it: CONCEDED, OUTCOME=INCORRECT — identical outcome statistics, demonstrating §N4's no-laundering property.)

**FIXTURE C — process-violating, prediction nevertheless correct.** Same type; decision predicts observable Y at H. During OPEN a well-formed challenge is filed; FCC's `fcc-response` misses the ratified response window → automatic conduct violation (Doctrine §I3/§M2, not a filing-time defect — the pre-registration is intact, so the instrument remains valid). At horizon the criterion is met → **TERMINAL: RESOLVED-CORRECT · OUTCOME=CORRECT · PROCESS=VIOLATED** · corrections entry filed for the window breach; the violation is displayed on the instrument and counted in the process-violation rate. The correct outcome does not erase the process failure — the exact separation the dual dimensions exist to enforce. Deterministic ✓. (Variant note: had the violation been filing-time — e.g., an undisclosed lock-time conflict — the walk ends INVALIDATED-BY-DOCTRINE · PROCESS=VIOLATED · OUTCOME=UNSCORED with `informational_outcome=CORRECT` published per §N3, so even invalidation cannot hide what happened.)

**Fixtures A–C re-run under v0.1.1:** all three walks are unchanged in disposition and scoring; each now additionally records `ordering_evidence.level = ANCHORED` (anchor confirmed within the 72h+ OPEN period, `anchor_blocktime` preceding each resolution measurement) and A/B/C render with independently proven precedence. Deterministic ✓ under the ratified rules, including the 7-day response window (B and C's response timing evaluated against it — C's breach is a breach of exactly that ratified window).

**FIXTURE D — lock claimed before an action; independent precedence evidence does not establish the ordering.** Type `qualification-stance`, horizon `filed_at + 80h` (legal: exceeds the 72h minimum OPEN). Walk: DRAFT → lint pass → FILED/LOCKED at T0 — `filed_at = T0` recorded (Layer 0: a claim), `lock_sha256` published to the public channel within the same operation (Layer 1: `publication_ref`, provider-attested timestamp ≈ T0), OTS anchor requested immediately at T0 (timely — no conduct violation). Bitcoin confirmation is delayed by network conditions and lands at **T0 + 120h** — *after* the resolution measurement at T0 + 80h. AI counter-thesis filed; no challenges. At horizon the criterion is met per the named source. The instrument may not terminate until the anchor confirms (§3.5); at T0 + 120h it terminates. Evaluation of `ordering_evidence` (§3.6): ANCHORED cannot be claimed for the ordering that matters — `anchor_blocktime` (T0+120h) does **not** precede the resolution measurement (T0+80h); the strongest independently established layer for that ordering is PUBLISHED. Resulting state: **TERMINAL: RESOLVED-CORRECT · OUTCOME=CORRECT · PROCESS=COMPLIANT (anchor requested timely; delay was the network's) · `ordering_evidence.level = PUBLISHED` · rendered permanently with "PRECEDENCE: NOT INDEPENDENTLY PROVEN," and counted outside the anchored-precedence share published with every aggregate.** The system records the correct outcome and simultaneously refuses to represent the lock-before-outcome ordering as independently proven — Layer-0 `requested_at` establishes nothing to a third party, and Layer-1 is only as strong as the channel's attestation, which is exactly what the label says. Deterministic ✓.
**Why the same evidence would be insufficient at Stage 1:** in Fixture D the gap between lock and the dependent event was 80 hours and anchoring *still* missed it; a Stage-1 capital action may follow its lock by minutes. Layer 2 can never reliably confirm inside such a window, and Layer 1 is attestation-grade, not cryptographic — a hosting provider's clock is testimony, not proof. So for real capital, no available layer demonstrates that the locked commitment existed before the dependent capital action, which is precisely why §3.8 leaves the Stage-1 attestation mechanism GATED rather than pretending `requested_at` proves it.

All four fixtures classify deterministically under the v0.1.1 rules as written; no rule ambiguity was encountered that required discretion to resolve.
